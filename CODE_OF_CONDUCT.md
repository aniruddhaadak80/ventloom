# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in ventloom and its community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible
or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion or veteran status.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, communicative,
welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behaviour includes:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologising to those affected by our mistakes
- Focusing on what is best for the community as a whole, not for ourselves alone

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- The use of sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information - a physical or email address -
  without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement

Incidents may be reported privately to the maintainers at
`aniruddha.adak@email.com` or through the security advisory channel. All
complaints are reviewed promptly and fairly, and every reporter's privacy is
protected.

The maintainers, who are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards, will take corrective action they deem appropriate, up to and
including a permanent ban.
